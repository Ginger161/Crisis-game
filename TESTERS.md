# CRISIS — tester guide

Thank you for testing. Crisis is a survival game about a hostel under siege. It runs in your phone's browser and installs like an app.

## Install (30 seconds)
- **iPhone (Safari):** open the link → Share → **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android (Chrome):** open the link → menu (⋮) → **Install app** / **Add to Home screen**.
Playing from the home-screen icon gives you the full screen. The game works offline once it has loaded once.

## How a session goes
You control one survivor (the one marked **you**). Tap the floor to walk. Tap an object when you are standing near it: it glows when it answers. Everyone else lives their own life. Tap a person to switch to them. Time runs on its own; the speed button (top right) changes it, and it pauses whenever a sheet is open.

## Please try to
1. Play for 10 minutes without reading anything. Note where you got stuck or confused.
2. Build something, cook something, go to sleep, send someone out, go out yourself.
3. Tap quickly and clumsily. Tap objects from across the room. Tap while someone walks.
4. Rotate your phone mid-game. Switch apps and come back. Lock the screen and come back.
5. Close the game and reopen it (the **Continue** button should bring you back to where you were).

## What to tell us
Use **☰ → Copy a problem report** the moment something feels wrong, then paste it into your message. It includes the day, the time, what everyone was doing, and the last few events. Please also tell us:
- Your phone and browser (for example "iPhone 13, Safari" or "Galaxy A14, Chrome").
- What you did just before it happened, in one sentence.
- A screenshot if something looks broken.

## Good questions to answer at the end
- Was there a moment you wanted to stop playing? What was happening?
- Did you ever not know what to do next?
- Which character did you care about, and why?
- Was anything too easy, too hard, or unfair?
- Was there any text you could not read, or a button you could not tap?

## Known limits (no need to report)
- The game is in English only.
- Sound starts after your first tap (phones require it).
- The campus map, the other survivor groups and the tutorial are still being expanded.
