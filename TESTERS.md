# CRISIS — tester guide

Thank you for testing. Crisis is a survival game about students trapped in a hostel while bandits hold the campus. It runs in your phone's browser and installs like an app. It is grim on purpose: people can get sick, get hurt, break down and die.

## Install (30 seconds)
- **iPhone (Safari):** open the link → Share → **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android (Chrome):** open the link → menu (⋮) → **Install app** / **Add to Home screen**.

Playing from the home-screen icon gives you the full screen. After the first load the game works offline and opens almost instantly.

## How it plays
- **By day, in the hideout.** You direct the whole group. Tap a face at the bottom to pick someone, then tap the floor to send them there or tap a marker to give them a job (a ring fills while they work). Someone on a long job is busy until it is done, and you move on to someone free; tap their face again to stop them. People you are not directing live their own life: they cook, sleep, smoke, argue and fall ill. Tap a selected face again to see how they are, in words.
- **On a phone held upright.** The house fills the top of the screen. The faces, the person you picked and their needs sit in a sheet at the bottom, with Items, World, Journal and End day under your thumb. Drag the sheet's handle up (or tap Items, World or Journal) to pull it up; drag it down or tap ✓ to put it back. The house keeps running above it.
- **The night plan** is a box for each place to be tonight: Bunks, Floor, Keep watch, Go out, Send out alone. Drag a face into a box, or tap the face and then the box. Buildings to go to are tiles: distance, then loot and danger as little bars.
- **At dusk, the night plan.** Choose who sleeps in a bed or on the floor, who keeps watch, and who goes out. The plan also tells you how cold the night will be.
- **At night, the run.** Walk your runner through a dark building. Tap the glowing spots to search. Noise spreads as rings, and bandits come to look where they heard it. Be home by 05:00.
- **At dawn, the morning report.** It tells you what happened while you were out.
- People knock at the door. A man called Mai Kudi comes to collect for the bandits. The radio, once you build one, changes what is out there.

## On a PC
Open the same link in a desktop browser (Chrome, Edge or Firefox) with the window at least 1100 wide. It switches to the PC layout on its own; Settings > Layout can force PC or Phone.
- The whole house is on screen. Your group is down the left: click a card (or press 1 to 6) to pick someone.
- Hover anything in the house: it says what it is and what a click will do. Click to do it. Right-click stops whoever you picked.
- Keys: Space pause, F speed, Tab next person, E end the day, I / W / J for Items, World, Journal, Esc to close. In a choice, press its number. On a run: S sneak, H home.
- Please tell us if anything feels like a phone screen stretched onto a monitor.

## Please try to
1. Play for 15 minutes without reading anything. Note where you got stuck or confused.
2. Build something, cook, make something at the workbench, and go out on at least two night runs.
3. Let someone get sick or hurt and try to look after them.
4. Tap quickly and clumsily. Rotate your phone mid-game. Switch apps and come back. Lock the screen and come back.
5. Close the game and reopen it. **Continue** should bring you back exactly where you were.

## If it feels slow
The game picks its graphics level for your phone automatically. You can change it under **☰ → Settings → Graphics** (Auto, Full, Balanced, Light). Please tell us which level it chose and whether it still felt slow.

## What to tell us
Press **☰ → Copy a problem report** the moment something feels wrong, then paste it into your message. If your phone will not copy it, the report appears on screen: press and hold, Select all, Copy. It includes the day, the time, what everyone was doing, and the last few events. Please also tell us:
- Your phone and browser (for example "iPhone 13, Safari" or "Galaxy A14, Chrome").
- What you did just before it happened, in one sentence.
- A screenshot if something looks broken.

If the title screen ever says your save "could not be read" or "is from an older version", tell us. The save is kept aside, not deleted, and we can recover it.

## Good questions to answer at the end
- Was there a moment you wanted to stop playing? What was happening?
- Did you ever not know what to do next?
- Which character did you care about, and why?
- Did a choice ever come back to you later? Did that feel fair?
- Was anything too easy, too hard, or unfair?
- Was there any text you could not read, or a button you could not tap?

## Known limits (no need to report)
- English only.
- Sound starts after your first tap (phones require it).
- The tutorial is still being written.
- A new version of the game may not be able to continue a save from an older one. The title screen will say so.
