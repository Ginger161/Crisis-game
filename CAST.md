# CRISIS — The cast (for review)

*66 named people in 14 groups, plus unnamed followers. Voices are hidden from players and listed here only so you can proof them. Everything below is invented.*

**How a run uses them.** Four of the *deep* characters are drawn at random as your party. Their group-mates stay behind in the group, so your people have friends, rivals and family across the campus. Anyone can later defect or be taken in.

**To change a voice**, edit `voice` in `cast.js` (deep characters) or the voice code in `groups.js` (supporting). The Pidgin rules and dialect word lists are in `voice.js` and are a first pass.
